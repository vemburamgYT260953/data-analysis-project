a# Employee Data Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset to understand why employees leave.

## Dataset
Source: Kaggle, IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance (1,470 rows, 35 columns).

## Business Questions
1. Which department and job role have the highest attrition?
2. Does overtime increase attrition?
3. How does income differ between leavers and stayers?
4. How do tenure and promotion gaps relate to attrition?
5. How do satisfaction and work-life balance relate to attrition?
